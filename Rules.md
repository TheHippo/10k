# The Dice Game 10,000

10,000 (also called Farkle or Zilch) is a fun, fast-paced dice game where players race to reach 10,000 points. Here's how it works:

## Basic Setup
- Players need 6 dice and a scoresheet
- Players take turns rolling dice
- The first to reach 10,000 points wins

## Scoring
Certain dice combinations earn points:

**Single dice:**
- **1** = 100 points
- **5** = 50 points
- Other numbers (2, 3, 4, 6) = 0 points

**Three of a kind** (three matching numbers):
- Three 1s = 1,000 points
- Three 2s = 200 points
- Three 3s = 300 points
- Three 4s = 400 points
- Three 5s = 500 points
- Three 6s = 600 points

**Four, five, or six of a kind** = For each additional die the points double. 

**Straights and other combos**:
- 1-2-3-4-5-6 = 1,500 points
- Three pairs = 750 points

## How a Turn Works
1. Roll all 6 dice
2. Set aside any dice that score points, but you must set aside at least one die.
3. You can either:
    - **Bank your points**: If you have at least 350 points you can end your turn, add your score to your total, and pass the dice
    - **Continue rolling**: Roll the remaining dice to try to score more (risky!)

4. If you roll dice with no scoring combinations or less than 350 points, you **"farkle"** or **"bust"**—you lose all points from that turn and pass the dice.
5. If you had three farkles in a row, you lose 1,000 points.

## Winning
The first player to accumulate 10,000 points while using all the dice wins. All players who hadn't had their turn get a chance to match the point in the last round.
